The trade plan

Work in this order: context, then structure, then zone. Wait for a significant bar. Put the stop where the scenario is wrong and the target where the orders are. Risk 1%, and send all three orders together. (Classic Part 7, PDF pp.191–226.)

0 · Context

Trade in favour of the larger structure. If you read accumulation, look for buys only. If you read distribution, look for sells only, or exits if you hold (pp.192–193).

1 · The three zones (accumulation; flip for distribution)

ZoneEntriesTrade-off
CThe Spring (only on low volume). The Spring’s Test (a favourite), which must hold above the Spring low.Best R:R, least confirmation
DA significant bar, or a minor LPS inside the range. The BUEC/LPS breakout test (Wyckoff’s favourite).Structure confirmed, less room left
EA significant bar, a pullback, a lower-timeframe re-accumulation, or an Ordinary Shakeout.Most secure, least room left

Earlier signal = more room, less reliability (p.198). Heavy Spring → wait for the test (pp.199–200). Phase C, D and E: pp.199–207.

2 · Trigger: the significant bar (pp.208–209)

  • A wider range than the bars before it
  • Higher volume
  • A close in the upper half (lower half for a bearish bar)
  • A close above a recent resistance (below a support)

Two ordinary bars together can count as one. Reversal of movement: control flips when a bar of the opposite intent closes beyond the far end of the last significant bar (pp.210–211).

3 · Stop: where the scenario fails (pp.216–220)

EntryLong stopShort stop
ShakeoutBelow the Spring lowAbove the UT high
Shakeout testBelow the SOS bar, or below the SpringAbove the SOW bar, or above the UT
Minor structureBelow its lowAbove its high
Breakout testBelow the SOS bar and the CreekAbove the SOW bar and the Ice

Alternative for a breakout test: a stop at mid-range (p.131). The book doesn’t use trailing stops (pp.220–221). Stocks can gap through a stop.

4 · Target: where the orders are (pp.221–226)

  • Liquidity zones: earlier highs and lows, and the structure’s edges (SC, AR, BC). After a Spring test, the first target is the Creek (p.130). Use the higher timeframe too, and move a target closer if a nearer zone appears.
  • Exit signals: a climactic bar, or a full Phase A on your timeframe.
  • Volume levels: VPOC, naked VPOC and VWAP (Lesson 11).

5 · Size and send (pp.212–214)

Risk per trade≤ 1% of the account
Shares(account × 1%) ÷ |entry − stop|
R:R|target − entry| ÷ |entry − stop|. Skip it if R:R < 1.
OrdersSend the entry, stop and target at the same time
