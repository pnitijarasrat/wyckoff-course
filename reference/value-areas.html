Trading with value areas

One question: where is price relative to the reference value area, and when it tries to get in, does it succeed? The rules are universal, whether the value area belongs to a candle, a session, a move or a structure (2.0 p.236).

The four principles

PrincipleWhen → tradeTarget · cancel
Trading rangeInside value, nothing changed → fade the edges: buy the VAL, sell the VAHMin: VPOC. Max: opposite edge · cancelled by acceptance outside
ReversionFrom outside, gets in and is accepted → trade back through value (trigger: inside test of the edge)Opposite edge, managed at the VPOC · cancelled if slammed at the VPOC
ContinuationTries to get in and is rejected at the edge (or before it) → trade away from value. The test after a breakoutNext HVN / liquidity zone · cancelled by acceptance back inside
Failed reversionGot in, strongly rejected at the VPOC → no trade; the reversion is cancelledBack out through the edge → continuation. Real break of the VPOC → reversion again

2.0 pp.237–245; summary diagrams p.246.

Always

  • Manage at the first relevant level. A VAL buy is managed at the VPOC: take a partial, or tighten the stop (p.245).
  • Confluence. Best triggers sit where levels line up: VAL + weekly VWAP + an older VPOC (p.238).
  • Hold both scenarios. The same level can be a buy or a short. What price does there decides it (p.240).
  • Plan levels (p.235): enter at VAH/VAL/VPOC/VWAP with a trigger. Stop beyond an LVN. Target at an HVN.

On daily stock charts

Book (intraday futures)You (daily stocks)
Previous session’s profileFixed range over the current Wyckoff range, or the previous month. The book suggests weekly or composite profiles for longer-term trading (p.237)
Opens away from valueGaps outside the value area (earnings!)
AcceptanceWorking rule, not the book’s: ≥ 2 daily closes inside, and the first pullback holds the edge from inside. One close = an attempt

Wyckoff mapping (course translation)

PrincipleWyckoff
Reversion from below / aboveSpring / UTAD that re-enters the range
ContinuationBUEC at the VAH, LPSY at the VAL
Continuation, short, after a dipA potential Spring that can’t get back above the VAL (p.223)

The “80% rule”

Reversion adapts Market Profile’s 80% rule (p.239): an intraday open outside value, then two 30-minute periods back inside → “80%” odds of crossing the value area. There’s no audited statistic. Treat the number as the rule’s name, not as odds. Source: marketcalls.
