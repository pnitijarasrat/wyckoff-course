Choosing the stock: five steps and a scan

Which chart do you read? Wyckoff’s five steps, the RS line, and a starting scan for Deepvue.

1 · The five steps (ChartSchool tutorial)

StepQuestionLook at
1Where is the market going?Index, weekly then daily (Lesson 10)
2Which stocks are in harmony with it?RS line: stronger in an up market, weaker in a down one
3Which have a cause big enough?A range near the top (acc. / re-acc.)
4Which are ready to move?The nine tests: Phase C done or D on
5When?A turn in the index. Stop placed

“Three-quarters or more of individual issues move in sync with the overall market.” Steps 1 and 5 are the market. Steps 2–4 are the stock. When unsure, drop the stock.

2 · The RS line

  • RS = stock close ÷ S&P 500 close. Rising = beating the market, even while the price falls.
  • In gear (Fraser): price above a rising 39-week (~200-day) average, and RS above its own 39-week average. Or price and RS making higher lows together.
  • Top-down: sector → group → stock. “The strongest stocks in the strongest groups.” Large caps lead first.
  • Inside a range: RS turning up from the ST into the SOS, and holding on the pullback to the LPS, is one more strength trace.

3 · Measure from the turn (my rule)

Rank RS after Step 1 says the market has turned, from the turn. Spring 2025, 45 large caps, the next four months:

RS ranked overTop thirdBottom third
The fall+4%+37%
7 weeks off the low+36%+3%

One episode, my own count. During a fall, an RS leader is often just money hiding in defensive names: one to watch, not one to buy.

4 · The routine

Market (your weekly read) → scan (30–80 names) → read (weekly glance → 5–10, full daily read → 1–3 plans) → timing (alert at the trigger, enter with the index).

5 · A starting scan (my recipe)

FilterStep
Price ≥ $10, average dollar volume ≥ $20M a day—
Price above a rising 200-day average2
Relative strength in the top 20–30%2
Within ~25% of the 52-week high3

Deepvue: start from the “Deepvue Leaders” or “Volatility Contraction” preset and stack the rest. The exact filters are on the Deepvue scan cheat sheet. A scan can’t do Step 4: no filter tells a Spring from an MSOW.

6 · The nine buying tests (Step 4)

  1. Downside objective reached (P&F count; use Lesson 8 targets)
  2. PS, SC, ST
  3. Volume up on rallies, down on reactions
  4. Downtrend (supply) line broken
  5. Higher lows
  6. Higher highs
  7. Stronger than the market
  8. Base forming (a horizontal range)
  9. Upside ≥ 3× the risk to the stop

The selling tests mirror them: upside objective reached, PSY and BC, lower highs and lows, weaker than the market, a crown forming, downside ≥ 3× the risk.
