The price cycle

The ten-second read

  1. Mark the last 3–4 swing highs and lows.
  2. Higher highs and higher lows → uptrend (markup).
    Lower highs and lower lows → downtrend (markdown).
    Overlapping in a band → range.
  3. For a range, look left. What trend came before?
  4. Name both possible structures. Don’t pick one until there’s evidence.

Trend rules

  • One pullback doesn’t break a trend. A lower low (in an uptrend) does.
  • A climax of very high volume after a long move, followed by a sharp reaction, is often where a trend stops and a range begins.

Long-only filter

ConditionBuy?
MarkupYes. Trade with it.
Range (either kind)Only with evidence (later lessons)
MarkdownNo, however cheap it looks

Remember

  • Not every range is a campaign. Some are noise.
  • Each timeframe has its own cycle. Check the weekly.
