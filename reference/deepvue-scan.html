The scan in Deepvue: exact filters

The Lesson 19 scan as exact Deepvue filters: field names as Deepvue spells them, with operator and value. Keep it open beside “Create New Screen”.

1 · How the screen builder combines filters

  • New Screen opens “Create New Screen”: 8 filter groups. Hover a filter for its tooltip, Add it, and it lands under Applied Filters.
  • Group 1 is ALL: a stock must pass every filter in it. This is where the scan lives.
  • Add Group makes an ANY group: a stock must pass at least one filter in each ANY group, after passing the ALL group. Use one for “show me any strength trace”.
  • To start from a preset: My Screens → Screener Presets, ⋮ → Duplicate, then Edit Filter.

Rows 4–5 are “above a rising 200-day”. Row 6: 70 = top 30%; tighten to 80 if you get more than ~80 names. Row 7 is top-down: the stock's industry in the top third. Row 9: 0 is tightest, 100 loosest; a recent gap makes ordinary days look tight.

4 · Which RS period? (Lesson 19's rule)

Market (Step 1)Use
Index has turned up, < 6 weeksRS Rating – 1 Month (21 days ≈ from the turn)
Uptrend under wayRS Rating – 3 Month (the default above)
Index still fallingWatch-list only: Relative Strength Days (%) – 20 ≥ 60

Avoid RS Rating 6 and 12 Month after a turn: they still score the fall, which ranks defensive names first. The RS Rating is a 1–99 rank against all Deepvue stocks, benchmarked to the S&P 500.

5 · Columns to add (a column set for reading the list)

RS Rating – 1 Month · RS Rating – 3 Month · Industry Rank – 3 Month · % Off the 52 Week High · RMV – 15 Days · Up/Down – 50 Day · A/D Rank · Stan Weinstein Stages · Average $ Volume – 50 Day

Sort by RMV – 15 Days ascending: the tightest ranges come first, which is where Phase C/D candidates sit.

8 · What Deepvue can't screen

  • The RS line against its own 39-week average (Fraser's “in gear”). The DV Mansfield RS chart indicator shows it (RS vs a 52-week average, zero line): check it on the chart, not in the scan.
  • A rising ATR vs a falling one. ATR % is a level, not a slope. RMV and 3 Weeks Tight stand in for “contracting”.
  • Step 4. No field tells a Spring from an MSOW. Stan Weinstein Stage 1 / 2A only says “basing” or “just left the base”.
  • Steps 1 and 5. The market read is yours. DV – Market Cycle on SPY is one input, not the read.
