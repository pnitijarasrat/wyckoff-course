Phases D and E

Phase D is the run from the Phase C test to the confirmed break of the Creek or Ice. Phase E is the trend outside the range. The breakout is only the alert. The trade is the quiet test after it.

A break starts at the Phase C extreme, with wide bars and rising volume. It should reach at least mid-range, and its pullbacks should stay beyond the Spring/UTAD (Classic pp.155, 161–165).

Three kinds of LPS (and LPSY)

  • After the shakeout: the test of the Spring.
  • Inside the range: the pullback after an SOS or mSOS.
  • Outside the range: the BUEC and every Phase E pullback.

Sometimes it forms at the PS (or PSY) level (Classic pp.174–177).

At the Creek: three outcomes

  1. An LPS inside the range, then another attack (“a run to jump the river”).
  2. A straight break, with large traders absorbing the supply.
  3. A failure, which becomes a new shakeout the other way (least likely).

Classic p.187.
